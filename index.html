02 / Before you deploy

Playtest protocol

01

Use a desktop browser

Keyboard and mouse are recommended. WebAssembly and WebGL 2.0 support are required.

02

Capture the cursor

Click inside the game to look around. Press Esc to release the mouse.

03

Test the mechanics

Move with WASD, fire with left click, aim with right click, and reload with R. MS-03 builds add Q to place an anchor and Left Shift to teleport.
